# Latered Support

## About Latered

Latered is a task management app designed for ADHD minds. It shows you when to *start* tasks, not just when they're due — helping you stay aware of time and avoid last-minute scrambles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a task?
Tap the input bar at the bottom of the screen, enter your task name, and follow the steps to set a due time and duration.

### What are urgency badges?
The colored badges show how soon you need to start a task based on its due time and estimated duration. This helps you see at a glance which tasks need attention now.

### How do recurring tasks work?
Create a routine in the Templates section. Set the days and times, and Latered will automatically add tasks to your schedule.

### How do Live Activities work?
When you start a task, a timer appears on your lock screen showing elapsed time. This helps you stay aware of how long you've been working.

### Is my data backed up?
All data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using iCloud device backups to preserve your data.

### Can I sync between devices?
Currently, Latered stores data locally on each device. Cross-device sync may be added in a future update.

## Troubleshooting

### Notifications aren't working
1. Go to Settings → Notifications → Latered
2. Ensure notifications are enabled
3. Check that "Time Sensitive" notifications are allowed

### Live Activities don't appear
1. Go to Settings → Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode)
2. Scroll down and enable "Live Activities"
3. Also check Settings → Latered → Live Activities

### Tasks disappeared
Tasks are organized by date. Check the Yesterday, Today, and Tomorrow tabs. Completed tasks appear in the completed section at the bottom.
